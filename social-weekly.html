Quick tip

Have a Facebook page? You still need a website.

  • You don't own your page.Facebook sets the rules and can change your reach overnight.
  • Google can't see most of it.Customers searching for you may never find it.
  • A website builds trust.New customers take a real site more seriously.
  • You can't collect leads there.No quote forms, no email list of your own.
pocketsod.com

A Facebook page is a great start, but it isn't a website. You don't own the page, Google can't see most of it, and you can't build an email list there. Your website is the one place online that's fully yours.   • Match your website exactly.Same name, address and phone everywhere you're listed.
  • Get reviews and answer them.Reply to every one, good or bad.
pocketsod.com

Want customers nearby to find you? It starts with a free Google Business Profile. Claim it, fill out every field, keep your details the same as your website, and reply to every review.
